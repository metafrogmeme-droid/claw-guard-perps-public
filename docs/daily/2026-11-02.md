## Why the stop was on

The 1 Nov wrap-up flattened the account with `pnpm cli kill`. That journal starts with a `kill_switch` trip receipt,
and anchoring it (tx 0x7636f28448f8b104beef07d34efa26431a4c1b46e9d64f82958b47818f0c5873, block 39131935,
21:54:47 CET) emitted `Stopped` on ClawGuardReceipts 0xeda7071961c518ce154062cb6d8e94219f8c29b3. No re-arm followed,
so the stop stayed on overnight. The operator notes had wrongly recorded it as off. The guard behaved as designed:
a stop anchored on chain holds until the owner re-arms it.

## Lesson for tomorrow

A manual `kill` that is anchored is a real on-chain stop, not a local one. After any wrap-up the operator must either
re-arm on purpose (`pnpm cli rearm runs/<wrapup>.jsonl`, with the owner's OK) or say plainly that the bot stays stopped.
Improvement: have `kill` print the on-chain stop state after anchoring, and have the preflight read `isStopped`
so a stop is reported before a session is launched.
