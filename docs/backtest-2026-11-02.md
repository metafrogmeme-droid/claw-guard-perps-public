# Backtest: skew maker vs band maker (2026-11-02)

Replay of 72 h of dreamDEX testnet 1-minute candles (BTC-PERP, ETH-PERP; public candle API) through the
real guard with SESSION_POLICY unchanged ($15 order, $400/day, $40 position, 2x, Needs your OK above $12,
200 bps drawdown stop). Cycle 120 s, quote $10.50. A resting order fills only when the candle trades through
it by `penaltyTicks`. Command: `pnpm exec tsx scripts/backtest.ts <candles.json> --strategy skew|band`.

Most of the edge came from the first half; the second half is roughly break-even.

## Caveats (read these)
- Candle replay is optimistic: no queue position, fills at our price when the minute trades through it.
- Recorded live book (15:28-15:39 CET today): book mid sat 10-23 bps ABOVE the mark on BTC and ETH,
  spread 1.5-2.3 bps. The strategy quotes around the mark, so live, the ask is close to the book and
  the bid far below it: expect one-sided fills (adverse selection). A book-anchored quote is the next fix.
- Testnet fees measured 0 so far; 2 bps stress shown.
- 72 h, 2 markets, a handful of parameter sets: not enough for any profit claim. Amounts are cents.
- The $400/day cap binds: about 38 opening quotes per day. Size is capped by your rules on purpose.
